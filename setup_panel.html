Optional AI analysis

Your profile and the listing data are sent to OpenAI. The OPENAI_API_KEY environment variable is required: the key is never entered or stored here. If you change the profile, listings are rated again on the next search.

Freshness and exchange rate

The ECB rate is read from ecb.europa.eu once a day and shown with its date. If it cannot be reached, the rate you entered is used.
